FAQ

Is 13th month pay taxable?

13th month pay and other benefits up to ₱90,000 are tax-exempt under TRAIN. Amounts beyond that join taxable compensation. This tool shows the accrued 13th month line separately so you can decide.

Why are the rates editable?

Statutory tables change (SSS MSC brackets, PhilHealth premium phases). Defaults follow current published rates, but you should always match your latest official contribution schedule — one edit, all computations update.

Does it handle semi-monthly pay?

The payslip is monthly; for semi-monthly runs, print two payslips and split (the tool shows monthly totals you can halve). Full semi-monthly mode is on the PRO roadmap.
